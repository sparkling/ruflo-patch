## PICK / HAND-PORT details

### HP-1 — `28eb57543` #2250 model-router escalation override (HIGH VALUE)
- **File:** `v3/@claude-flow/cli/src/ruvector/model-router.ts`, `selectModel()` lines 644-655.
- **Bug (live in fork):** `uncertainty = max(0, 1 - scoreSpread - confidence*0.5)` is structurally ~0.6-0.7 for trivial routes; the gate `if (uncertainty > maxUncertainty=0.15 && bestModel !== 'opus')` fires on ~every low-complexity task → haiku→sonnet, sonnet→opus. Net: opus ~40% on trivial tasks, haiku never selected. The fork's ADR-0278 contextual bandit makes this strictly worse — a confidently-suppressed opus prior is escalated INTO, defeating the learned suppression.
- **Port:** replace the unconditional escalation (lines 649-653) with upstream's guarded version — skip escalation when EITHER (a) the escalation target's mean is worse than the selected model's mean by ≥0.10, OR (b) the selected model has a confident+decent posterior (α+β ≥ 5 AND mean ≥ 0.45). Cold-start Beta(1,1) fails both → preserves current conservative behavior. Add `CLAUDE_FLOW_MAX_UNCERTAINTY` env override (rename to `RUFLO_MAX_UNCERTAINTY` per fork branding). Bring the regression test (`issue-2250-2251-regression.test.ts`: load suppressed opus prior α=3.8/β=17.2, route 50 trivial, assert opusRate<0.20 ∧ haiku>0; cold-start preservation; env override).
- **Conflict risk:** LOW. Fork's `selectModel` is the same shape; the only fork-specific extra is the ADR-0280 γ-uplift blend (lines 626-631) which sits BEFORE the sort and is orthogonal to the escalation guard. Need the model means available at the escalation point — derivable from the Beta priors already in scope (`haikuP`/`sonnetP`/`opusP`).

### HP-2 — `28eb57543` #2251 daemon trigger headless race
- **File:** `v3/@claude-flow/cli/src/services/worker-daemon.ts` (`:353` fire-and-forget `initHeadlessExecutor()`, `:362` the init method, `:1853` `triggerWorker`).
- **Bug (live in fork):** constructor kicks off `initHeadlessExecutor().catch(...)` fire-and-forget; on-demand `daemon trigger -w <worker>` constructs a daemon and immediately calls `triggerWorker()`, racing init → `headlessAvailable` still false → local-stub fallthrough in ~2ms. (Scheduled fires on a long-running daemon don't race.)
- **Port:** capture the init promise into a private `headlessInitPromise` field; `await this.headlessInitPromise` at the top of `triggerWorker()`. ~3 lines. (`daemon trigger -w learn` is the deterministic learner driver per ADR-0290 — this race directly undermines that path.)
- **Conflict risk:** LOW (additive field + one await). Confirm the fork's `triggerWorker` entry (`:1853`) and the headless-worker dispatch branch (`:1396`).
